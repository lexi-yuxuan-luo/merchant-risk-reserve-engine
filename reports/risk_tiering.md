# Risk tiering results

Generated by `src/risk_model.py`. All data is synthetic.

## Setup

- Monthly point-in-time snapshots; target = merchant fails within the next 90 days.
- Train: snapshots [60, 90, 120, 150] (19,754 merchant-snapshots, 262 failures).
- Out-of-time test: snapshots [240, 270] (9,651 merchant-snapshots, 94 failures).
- Snapshots between the two are left out: their 90-day outcomes would not yet be known on day 240, when the model would go live.
- Counts are merchant-snapshots, not unique merchants: one failure can appear in up to three consecutive snapshots.

## Out-of-time tiers

## Early-warning overlay (gradient boosting)

Merchants the scorecard places in tiers A, B, C are flagged for review when the gradient boosting PD is above its own tier-E cut-off. Out-of-time results:

- Merchant-snapshots in tiers A-C: 7,724
- Flagged: 122 (1.6%)
- Failure rate, flagged: 5.74%; not flagged: 0.54%
- Failures caught: 7 of 48 in these tiers

## Exposure (EAD) check

EAD is the run-rate exposure (undelivered sales likely to be disputed + sales still inside the normal dispute window), scaled by a factor fitted on training-period failures, similar to a credit conversion factor. The check below is on test-period failures only.

- Calibration factor (fitted on training failures): 1.15
- Failures: 94
- Sum of calibrated EAD: $559,231
- Sum of realized loss: $698,817
- Realized / calibrated estimate: 1.25
- Rank correlation (Spearman): 0.93
