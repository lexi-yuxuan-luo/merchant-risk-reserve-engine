# Risk tiering results

Generated by `src/risk_model.py`. All data is synthetic.

## Setup

- Monthly point-in-time snapshots; target = merchant fails within the next 90 days.
- Train: snapshots [60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210] (29,501 merchant-snapshots, 396 failures).
- Out-of-time test: snapshots [240, 270] (9,651 merchant-snapshots, 94 failures).

## Exposure (EAD) check

EAD is the run-rate exposure (undelivered sales likely to be disputed + sales still inside the normal dispute window), scaled by a factor fitted on training-period failures, similar to a credit conversion factor. The check below is on test-period failures only.

- Calibration factor (fitted on training failures): 1.15
- Failures: 94
- Sum of calibrated EAD: $561,276
- Sum of realized loss: $698,817
- Realized / calibrated estimate: 1.25
- Rank correlation (Spearman): 0.93
